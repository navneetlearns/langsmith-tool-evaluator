6 · Limits and trust — can I trust and reproduce this?

Single judge, 30 queries, one run. The L-levels come from one in-session model (deepseek-v4) in two self-consistent passes — Grades come from one in-session model (deepseek-v4, a different family from the producing agent gpt-5.4-mini) in two passes: Phase 3 = direct reading against pre-written accountant skeletons (no separate LLM judge invocation, no second reader); Phase 2 = an anchored judge pass with a verbatim-evidence requirement. The two passes agree 20/20 — that is model SELF-CONSISTENCY, not human calibration... A human has not reviewed the ratings (human-reviewed 0/20). Grading measures element presence against the accountant skeletons more than numeric correctness of the figures (the consistency ledger cross-checks figures against each other and the known baseline, not against ground truth). The clarify gate is stochastic (~30% park rate on clarify-prone inputs), so per-query verdicts on parks are probabilistic, not categorical. q20's error is an upstream drop, not a verdict on the agent.
What this page does not claim: no claim that figures are ground-truth correct; no fabrication guarantee — "no fabricated figures detected (1 judge, 20 answers)" is the honest form; no estimate of run-to-run variance; no coverage of multi-turn behavior or other workspaces. The "44.2s average" of the old readout is not used — latency is shown by outcome. FinGAIA (arXiv:2507.17186v2) relied primarily on manual review of every answer, supplemented by LLM-as-judge; this run is the opposite — LLM-only grading, 0/20 human-reviewed — so label, tier and error-code values here carry that confidence gap.
